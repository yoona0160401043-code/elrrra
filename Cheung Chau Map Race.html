Cheung Chau Map Race

Groups
How to play: Each group needs a mini whiteboard or paper. Click Next card. Groups write the answer before time runs out and hold it up. Click Show answer, tap every group that got it right, then Move ships. Ships sail 1 or 2 stops. First ship to the treasure wins!
玩法：每組準備白板或紙。按 Next card 出題，各組在限時內寫答案並舉起。按 Show answer，再點選答對的組，按 Move ships，答對的船前進 1 或 2 格。最先到達寶藏的組勝出！
